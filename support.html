Ask us

Answers arrive in about two days, weekends aside

Write to nastya.shchaveleva@internet.ru, or tap Write to us inside Settings so your version details tag along.

I bought Pro but it shows as locked.

Open Settings, go to Membership and press Restore purchases, using the same Apple account that paid. If it reports nothing found, email a screenshot of your receipt and we will dig in.

How do I stop the subscription?

Through iPhone Settings, your name at the top, Subscriptions, then Horlo. Removing the app does not end billing.

My fast did not start by itself.

Scheduled starts need notifications allowed and the Start fasts automatically switch on under Schedule (a Pro feature). iOS can only launch alerts, so tap the notification to open the timer if it is not already open.

The countdown is missing on my Lock Screen.

Live Activities must be allowed for Horlo in iPhone Settings. Start a fast from the app once more and look again.

I logged a fast by mistake.

Find it in the Log and use the trash button labelled Delete fast.

Can I move my journal to a new phone?

Entries stay on the device they were made on and Horlo has no export or cloud copy, so a new iPhone starts with an empty journal unless you restore a full device backup.

Do you see my weight or mood?

No. They are stored on your phone only. Details are on Your data.
