The rules

Updated October 6, 2026

Please read first

Horlo is a timer and a journal, not medical advice. The fasting stages it shows are broad estimates drawn from published research, and your body may not match them. Skip fasting, or ask a clinician first, if you are pregnant or nursing, have diabetes, have a history of disordered eating, are underweight, take medication that must go with food, or are under 18. Stop and eat if you feel unwell.

Using Horlo

We license you to run Horlo on your own Apple devices for personal use. No copying, reverse engineering, reselling or building a clone from it. Apple's standard end-user licence also covers the app; where it disagrees with this page on a point it covers, Apple's wording wins.

Horlo Pro: plans and billing

  • Horlo Pro is offered as a yearly plan (with a free trial for new subscribers), a monthly plan, and a one-time lifetime purchase. What you pay and how long any trial lasts is spelled out on the screen where you buy, in your own currency.
  • Yearly and monthly plans renew automatically through your Apple ID. Charges happen up to 24 hours ahead of each period's end unless renewal was turned off before that.
  • Lifetime is paid once and does not renew.
  • Manage or cancel under iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Horlo's Settings screen shows where to go when Pro is active.
  • If a trial ends without cancelling, the plan begins and you are billed.
  • Apple handles refunds; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • New phone? Use Restore purchases; you will not be charged again.
