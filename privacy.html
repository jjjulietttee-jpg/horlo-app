Your data

Updated October 6, 2026

In one breath

Fasts, water, weight and mood are written to storage on your iPhone only. Notifications are scheduled by the phone itself. The one outside service is Adapty, which tells the app whether Horlo Pro is switched on.

What lives on your phone

  • Fasts: start, end, the plan you picked (such as 16:8) and your schedule settings.
  • Journal: glasses of water, body weight entries, and mood marks, plus your daily water goal.
  • Widget and Live Activity: the running fast is shared with Horlo's own widget through a private app group so the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island can show the countdown. That sharing happens within your device.

None of this is sent to us. Horlo does not link to Apple Health and asks for no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, Horlo asks iOS to ring at your chosen times: when a fast begins, when the goal is reached, and shortly before the eating window shuts. These are local alerts created on the device, so there is no push server, and you can switch them off in iPhone Settings, then Notifications, then Horlo.

The one outsider: Adapty

Buying Pro goes through Apple. Horlo then asks Adapty (adapty.io), a subscription service, to check the receipt. Adapty gets the receipt data, an anonymous profile ID, app and OS versions, the device type and storefront country, and a few numbers about the app's setup flow. We use that so Pro unlocks and stays unlocked, and to see in aggregate which screens lose people. Your fasting log and journal are not part of it.

There are no ad networks in Horlo and nothing that tracks you across other apps or sites.

If you write to us

The Write to us button in Settings opens a draft email that already lists build number, system release and your Adapty profile ID, so we can trace problems. Trim anything you prefer not to send. We use your message only to answer it.

Taking things off

  • One fast: open the Log and press the trash icon marked Delete fast.
  • All of it: remove Horlo from your iPhone, which erases the fasts, journal and settings stored here.
  • Adapty profile: send us an email and we will request its removal.

Age and updates

Horlo is meant for people 18 and older, since fasting is not advised for kids and teens. When we edit this page, the stamp at the top moves.
